## Findings

No additional concrete security or behavior blocker was found in the reviewed
slice. No product repair was made by this checker.

- The neutral command policy is the executable catalog/argument/confirmation
  owner for both adapters. Native never supplies a raw executable or shell;
  destructive typed-only catalog entries remain disabled. Tauri retains its
  existing DTO/TS definitions, names and export paths.
- Shared process resolution receives a consumer-owned `SidecarContext`.
  Tauri passes its own executable, package version, compile-time hash, debug
  policy and repository root; native supplies its corresponding context.
  Release identity still requires an adjacent sidecar with a matching hash,
  followed by version compatibility; no PATH fallback was introduced.
- Tauri delegates retained jobs, cancellation tokens, pruning and execution
  to `CommandJobs`, rather than keeping its old independent runner. The
  background runtime-policy permit is retained until runner completion.
  The bridge subscribes before polling execution, emits progress deltas, drains
  remaining progress and emits a terminal snapshot after cleanup. TimedOut
  preserves the old wire vocabulary as Failed plus `process_timeout`.
- Admission and cancellation capability share one lock. Cancel is a request;
  the runner owns immutable completion after managed-tree wait/termination and
  reader joins. CleanupFailed takes precedence. Queued native tickets have a
  drop guard if runtime admission/shutdown skips their work. View detachment
  leaves the app-owned runner alive.
- Output readers cap physical lines before batching; queues, stored lines and
  stored bytes are bounded. Snapshot and emitted delta text use the existing
  core redactor. The reproduced assignment leak was repaired in that existing
  owner, not through a native-only sanitizer. The additional four-byte UTF8
  regression exercises marker insertion at the capped boundary.
- Resource IDs resolve through the shared fixed registry. Native resolves
  capability/environment before default-root access, retains captured roots
  and the original snapshot token, checks session identity before saving, and
  delegates guarded backup/write policy. Missing-create and stale-save remain
  conflicts instead of overwrites. Scoped plaintext has no Debug/Serialize.
- The resource view uses render-owned generation-guarded confirmation entry,
  keeps drafts on conflict and clears session/editor/pending content on leave.
  Shell navigation invokes resource leave and job suspend, then loads/status-
  recovers the destination. Native source/IO remains outside render.
