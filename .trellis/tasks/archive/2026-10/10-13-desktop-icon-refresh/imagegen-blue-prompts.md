# Revised Blue Icon Candidates

Built-in image_gen, entirely new generation. Previous warm-white branching design rejected. User requests blue, modern simplicity inspired by premium technology identity without copying trademarks.

## A - Interlocking Geometric Mark
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset: one production-quality CCR desktop application icon, square image.
Design a completely NEW original brand symbol. CCR is a precise developer utility for organizing and switching configurations across AI command-line tools. Express this through a compact, confident abstract geometric emblem, not a literal diagram.
Art direction: the iconic simplicity and disciplined negative space of premium contemporary technology identity, with the finish quality of a native macOS app icon. Do not imitate Apple, Anthropic or OpenAI logos.
Composition: a large perfectly smooth pale icy-white squircle tile, straight-on orthographic view, centered with equal small exterior margins. On it, ONE centered cobalt-blue architectural emblem occupying roughly 56 percent of the tile. The emblem is formed from TWO broad, interlocking rounded angular ribbons, like two softly squared C-shaped brackets turning into each other. They create one beautifully balanced compact silhouette with a generous square negative-space opening and two deliberate breaks that suggest switching between configuration states. It should read as an original abstract monogram, not as linked chains or a padlock. Few large shapes, exact geometry, restrained corner radii, beautifully balanced proportions. Cobalt and royal blue, with only a whisper of lighter blue on a single folded surface to clarify the two planes. Mostly flat, no dramatic extrusion. No tiny features.
The pale blue-white base must remain clean and light. Blue is the only hue. No amber, no black, no warm beige.
Rendering: extraordinarily clean smooth edges, sharp vector-like silhouette, no texture, no grain, no distressed edge, no dramatic lighting. Discreet native-app material polish, not glass or a toy.
Outside tile genuinely transparent, absolutely clean alpha edge, no particles, no halos, no external shadow.
Avoid: text, letters printed on the tile, arrows, branching routes, nodes, Y shapes, network diagrams, terminal windows, command prompt glyphs, mascots, hexagonal woven knots, vendor trademarks, watermarks, mockup scenes, presentation boards. Only a single isolated polished icon.

## B - Folded C
Use case: logo-brand.
Create ONE original premium minimalist desktop application icon for CCR, a sophisticated tool that organizes and switches AI CLI configurations.
Completely disregard all previous icons. Aim for the clarity, confidence and optical discipline of world-class contemporary technology branding without reproducing any existing Apple, Anthropic, OpenAI, Linear or other trademark.
Use a very pale cool blue-white solid full-bleed square canvas. NO rounded-square outer tile, NO transparent cutout, NO mockup, NO border: the image itself is the icon source, with uniform pale background all the way to all four edges. Production rounding is applied later by the application asset pipeline.
Center a single cobalt-blue compact sculptural geometric C-shaped symbol, occupying 55 percent of canvas. The symbol is an elegant thick softly squared open loop with an opening to the right; it is made from one continuous broad ribbon. At the upper-right opening the ribbon has one precise diagonal folded cut, exposing a small lighter blue face. The lower-right end is a simple clean flat terminal aligned to the same geometry. This subtle fold should suggest changing configuration states without literal arrows. Huge clear inner negative space, mathematically disciplined proportions, instantly legible at 16 pixels. Original abstract C monogram, no wordmark.
Palette: vivid deep cobalt blue #1558EA, a small azure blue folded face, cool off-white #F3F7FC canvas. Only blue hues. Mostly flat with one very restrained depth cue at the fold. No gradients on the canvas, no shadows, no dramatic extrusion, no glass, no texture, no grain. Clean precise vector-like silhouettes. Beautifully balanced whitespace.
Absolutely no branches, nodes, cables, networks, tree icons, dots, amber, black, mascots, UI screens, printed text, ornaments, concentric rings, woven knots, or company logos. One large isolated icon source on its simple solid light square background.
